Naava’s website — three design directions

Same content and pages in each; only the design differs. Every page has a bar at the top to jump between mockups.

Mockup A: Paper & InkWarm and cozy: cream paper, handwritten touches, sculptures shown like snapshots pinned to a studio wall.Mockup B: GalleryCalm and professional: lots of white space, elegant type, every sculpture on an even grid like a gallery catalog.Mockup C: Playful StudioBright and joyful: colors pulled from her sculptures, rounded shapes, bouncy type — as fun as the art itself.

Featured pieces on the home pages are placeholders until Naava picks her favorites. The contact form is disabled in the mockups.
